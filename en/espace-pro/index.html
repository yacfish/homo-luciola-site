Presentation

Homo-Luciola is a collective creation by Cie LaterrateraL and Fōz Machine.

A hybrid show and performance in which the verticality of the dancing body enters into dialogue with the interactive Fōz Machine installation. Ascending and suspended movements become luminous vectors projected by the Fōz Machine, creating an ephemeral visual writing in the vertical space.

It is a creation at the crossroads of vertical dance, visual and digital arts, the audiovisual, parietal art and urban arts.

Current state of the work

A form of about 40 minutes is already viable: the audience sees something beautiful and satisfying, while knowing that many elements can still be improved. This form is not fixed. Work continues on the writing, the dramaturgy, the choreography, the sound and the image, including by modifying existing tableaux and by creating new ones (this has already been done for the last performance).

For touring, shorter forms of a quarter of an hour to 20 minutes can be assembled from the tableaux, adapted to a festival, a theme, a façade or a venue. One, two or three tableaux can be kept according to the context.

Sources: working exchange of 16 November 2026 (recordings 1 and remarks by Yacine and his collaborator).

Technical rider

The figures below bring together the technical riders already drawn up (residency rider, short rider, September 2026 rider) and the needs currently retained for the pack. When a value is not the same from one rider to another, or is missing, it is given with [à confirmer].

Format

In situ performance, vertical dance and interactive installation. Audience: all audiences. Capacity adapted to the space.

Duration

About 40 minutes for the main form, and short forms of 8 to 15 minutes. The indicative fee given for the full format is the same for these two durations.

Useful vertical height

8 to 10 m. The three technical riders agree on this range.

Rigging height

Above the useful height: +1 to 5 m, or +2 to 5 m according to the riders [à confirmer].

Width

About 10 m.

Minimum depth

7 m or 10 m according to the riders, plus the space for the audience [à confirmer].

Rigging points

Need currently retained for this pack: at least 4 points with a resistance of 500 kg [à confirmer]. The technical riders typically describe 3 points to hang the truss, plus guy-line and counterweight points, with a minimum resistance of 5 kN per point (about 500 kg). The detail of the rigging plan depends on the venue and remains [à confirmer].

Darkness

Strong darkness or blackout, needed for the light capture of the Fōz Machine. One rider also indicates an acceptable twilight for the lighting. Touring is planned at night. [à confirmer]

Electrical supply

220 V, with distribution points close to the Fōz Machine and to the control booth. The total power in kW depends on the size of the projection and has not yet been firmly quantified. For projectors of about 20,000 to 24,000 lumens, the need for a three-phase supply is not established.

Setup time

For a performance, allow three to four days on site: one day of setup, one night of rehearsal, one afternoon of rehearsal and one evening of performance. If the organiser has to hire a video projector, the hire also covers these three or four days. Setup of the vertical structure: one day, the installation and calibration of the Fōz Machine (half a day) being included in that day. If the company’s rigging systems cannot be put in place, the organiser hires a scaffolding firm to install a secure scaffold under the supervision of the two riggers.

Touring company

Full format as communicated: up to six people (three vertical dancers, one vertical dancer and two other artists), including Yacine. Tighter formats are possible (two vertical dancers, without a musician). Noé is a vertical dancer and rigger technician.

Lighting

The video projector is provided by the organiser. Rule communicated: about 1,000 lumens per metre of width (for example 20,000 lumens for a 20 m stage), and a little more if the walls are black or highly absorbent. The number and type of additional projectors still vary according to the technical riders already written.

Sound

Sound system provided by the organiser. One rider describes a 2.2 system, a stereo or façade diffusion, and 2 monitors [à confirmer le détail selon le lieu].

Site visit

A feasibility study is planned in advance, according to the site. Rigging plans, diagrams and safety or insurance documents: on request.

Logistics

Vehicle access and free parking for 3 to 4 vehicles, secure storage for the equipment, secure heated dressing rooms with sanitary facilities. Barriers around the performance area, and security if the space is public. One rider mentions catering for 6 people, including 2 vegetarians [à confirmer].

Touring conditions

Up to six people on tour (three vertical dancers, one vertical dancer and two other artists), for whom hotel and meals must be provided. Tighter formats are possible (two vertical dancers, without a musician). The equipment travels in a van; a van and a car are used. Each person sets out from home; the starting point is around Ath (Belgium), with Yacine coming from Brussels. A performance requires three to four days on site.
